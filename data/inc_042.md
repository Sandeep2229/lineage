# Incident Postmortem INC-042: Orders write bottleneck
Severity: SEV-1
Date: 2026-06-12
On-call: James Okafor (SRE)
Service: orders-service

## Summary
During the Summer Sale preview, orders-service writes hit 9k per second. The single Postgres primary saturated, lock contention spiked, and checkout failed for 38 minutes.

## Root cause
The Postgres single-primary design from ADR-001 cannot absorb burst writes above roughly 5k per second.

## Action items
Evaluate a horizontally scalable datastore for orders. Owner: Priya Shah.
