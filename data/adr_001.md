# ADR-001: Use Postgres as the primary datastore
Status: Accepted (later superseded by ADR-007)
Date: 2026-03-01
Decided by: Priya Shah (Head of Infrastructure)
Affects: orders-service, billing-service

## Context
Nimbus needs a primary datastore for orders and billing. The team knows SQL well and wants strong consistency.

## Decision
Use Postgres (single primary, two read replicas) for orders-service and billing-service.

## Consequences
Simple joins and transactions. Write throughput is bounded by a single primary. Revisit if writes exceed 5k per second.
