# ADR-007: Migrate orders-service to DynamoDB
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-03
Decided by: Priya Shah
Supersedes: ADR-001 (for orders-service only)
Motivated by: INC-042

## Decision
orders-service moves to DynamoDB with on-demand capacity. No new Postgres tables may be created for orders-service. billing-service remains on Postgres under ADR-001.

## Owner
Marcus Lee owns the migration, due end of Q4 2026.
